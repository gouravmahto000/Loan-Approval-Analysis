# Loan-Approval-Analysis
Customers with credit scores above 700 have a higher approval rate.
Salaried and business customers receive more approvals.
Loan-to-income ratio strongly affects approval.
Previous defaults increase rejection probability.
Higher income and higher credit scores are associated with approval.
